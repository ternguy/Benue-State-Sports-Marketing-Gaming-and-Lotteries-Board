Careers

Join our team and help shape the future of Sports Marketing, Gaming and Lottery Administration.

The Benue State Sports Marketing, Gaming, and Lotteries Board consists of a vibrant and dynamic leadership as well as talented, passionate and patriotic professionals determined to make a difference in service delivery and the development of Benue State.

As part of its strategies, BSMGLB has in place measures to attract, recruit, retain and motivate personnel who are determined to develop themselves, the State, and the country generally. It is a primary concern of BSMGLB to create a conducive working environment to ensure prompt and satisfactory service delivery.

As part of our recruitment policies, BSMGLB seeks individuals with integrity, abundant talent, exceptional work ethics and strength of character. Recruitment is based on good communication skills, ability to work as a team player and independently when required.

For an opportunity to join the BSMGLB team, send your resume in MS Word or PDF format to:

careers@bsmglb.be.gov.ng
