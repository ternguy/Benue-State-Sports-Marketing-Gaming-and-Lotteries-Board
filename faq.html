What is BSMGLB?

The Benue Sports Marketing, Gaming, and Lotteries Board regulates sports, marketing, gaming, and lotteries activities in Benue State.

How do I obtain a gaming licence?

Applications are submitted online together with all required documents.

How long does approval take?

Approval depends on successful verification and payment.

Can I renew online?

Yes. Existing licence holders can renew online through the portal.

Who can I contact?

Please contact our customer support for assistance.
