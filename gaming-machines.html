GAMING MACHINE

A Gaming Machine is any electronic, electro-mechanical or mechanical machine constructed or adapted for gaming. A player pays to play on the machine either by coin insertion or token and the outcome of the game is determined by the machine using a Random Number Generator certified by registered and recognized Gaming Labs.

An application for a gaming license machine is made to BSMGLB and the license issued upon submission and approval of the required documents. A Gaming machine license is valid for one (1) year and may be renewed prior or on its expiration.
