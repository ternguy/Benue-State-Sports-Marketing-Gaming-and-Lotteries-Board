Global Affiliations

As part of our commitment to world-class regulatory standards and international best practices, the Benue State Marketing Gaming and Lotteries Board (BSMGLB) is proud to be an active member of the International Association of Gaming Regulators (IAGR), the International Masters of Gaming Law (IMGL), and the International Association of Gaming Advisors (IAGA).

These global affiliations reflect our dedication to continuous learning, policy innovation, and global collaboration in the regulation and oversight of gaming activities. By aligning with these internationally recognized bodies, BSMGLB remains at the forefront of emerging trends in the gaming industry, strengthens regulatory frameworks, and promotes responsible gaming within Lagos State.

Our Membership Means

  • Access to a global network of regulators, advisors, and legal experts
  • Informed policy formulation and improved regulatory oversight
  • Active participation in global discussions on innovation and responsible gaming
  • Enhanced credibility and transparency in regulatory practices

BSMGLB continues to position Lagos State as a leading hub for transparent, inclusive, and technology-driven gaming regulation, both in Africa and globally.
