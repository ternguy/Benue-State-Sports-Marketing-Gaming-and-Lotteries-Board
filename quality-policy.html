POLICY STATEMENT

Policy Statement

Welcome to the Benue State Sports Marketing, Gaming and Lotteries Board. The Board is the statutory agency responsible for the regulation, promotion, supervision, and oversight of all sports marketing, gaming, betting, and lottery activities within Benue State. Our policies are founded on the principles of transparency, fairness, accountability, and good governance. We are committed to establishing a modern, efficient, and well-regulated industry that creates value for investors, operators, players, and the people of Benue State. Our Core Values i. ii. iii. Transparency: We conduct our activities with openness and honesty. All processes relating to licensing, compliance, regulation, monitoring, and revenue collection are designed to be clear, accessible, and accountable to the public. Accountability: We accept full responsibility for our actions and decisions. We are committed to ensuring that all revenues generated from the sector are properly accounted for and utilised in a manner that advances the development objectives of Benue State. Integrity: We uphold the highest ethical standards in all our operations. We are dedicated to maintaining a fair and competitive environment in which all operators, investors, and stakeholders are treated equitably and in accordance with the law. iv. Innovation: We embrace technological advancement and digital transformation to improve service delivery, strengthen regulatory efficiency, enhance compliance monitoring, and keep pace with emerging trends within the gaming and lottery industry. Our Regulatory Principles i. ii. iii. Fair Play: We ensure that all gaming, betting, and lottery activities conducted within Benue State are fair, transparent, random, and free from manipulation or unethical practices. Consumer Protection: We are committed to protecting players and participants from fraudulent practices and unlicensed operators. We also ensure that legitimate winnings are paid promptly and that operators comply with established regulatory standards. Responsible Gaming: We promote responsible participation in gaming activities and are committed to raising public awareness of the risks associated with problem gambling. We actively support measuresdesigned to protect minors, vulnerable persons, and communities from gambling-related harm. Our Commitment to Investors and Operators The Benue State Sports Marketing, Gaming, and Lotteries Board is open for business and committed to creating a stable, predictable, and investorfriendly regulatory environment. Under the current administration, the Board is implementing reforms aimed at improving ease of doing business, strengthening regulatory certainty, and encouraging responsible investment within the sector. We welcome partnerships with credible local and international investors, operators, and development partners who share our vision of building a thriving gaming and lottery industry. We assure all investors that their investments will be protected and governed by transparent, consistent, and enforceable laws and regulations. Our Commitment to the People of Benue State We recognise that the Board exists to serve the people of Benue State. Accordingly, all our activities are directed towards generating revenue that supports sports development, youth empowerment, infrastructure improvement, job creation, and social welfare programmes across the State. We are committed to managing public resources prudently, promoting public confidence in the sector, and providing regular updates on our achievements, initiatives, and contributions to the development of Benue State. Contact Us Should you require further information regarding our policies, regulatory framework, licensing procedures, or investment opportunities, we encourage you to contact the Benue State Sports Marketing, Gaming, and Lotteries Board. Together, we can build a vibrant, transparent, responsible, and prosperous gaming and lottery industry that contributes meaningfully to the sustainable development of Benue State.

Dr. Joseph S. Nule, FERIN, MTRCN, MTEPAN, MAVTEN

Executive Secretary

Benue State Sports Marketing, Gaming, and Lotteries Board.
