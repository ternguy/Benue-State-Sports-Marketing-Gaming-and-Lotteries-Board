Scratch Card & Interactive Games

Licensed Gaming Activities and Regulatory Information

Scratch Cards

A Scratch Card is an instant-win form of lottery game. Scratch cards are made from specially printed cardboard containing sealed prize areas. Players scratch off the concealed surface to reveal symbols, pictures, numbers, or words that determine whether they have won a prize.


With advancements in technology, scratch card games are now available online and through mobile devices, making participation easier and more accessible to players.

Interactive Games

Interactive games involve active participation between one or more players. The rules, gameplay structure, and operating procedures are determined by the license holder and are subject to approval by the relevant gaming regulatory authority.


These games may include digital competitions, skill-based contests, online engagements, and other approved gaming activities designed to provide entertainment and excitement for participants.

License Validity

Scratch Card and Interactive Gaming licenses are valid for a period of one (1) year and may be renewed before or upon expiration in accordance with regulatory requirements.

Online Access

Play approved games through web and mobile platforms.

Licensed Operations

All activities operate under approved gaming regulations.

Annual Renewal

Licenses remain active for one year and are renewable.
